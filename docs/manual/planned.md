# Planned features

What Symphony is working towards, grouped by area. Nothing here is in the
current preview. Order within a group is roughly the order it's likely to
arrive, but plans change; the live plan is in the repository's
[roadmap](../ROADMAP.md) and [feature direction](../plans/feature-direction.md).

The long-term goal is everything Harmony does, plus the additions below.

## Drawing and paint

- **Ink tool**, **gradient and texture swatches**, and **palette lists**
  you can share between scenes.
- **Symmetry**: horizontal and vertical first, then radial and arbitrary.
- **Guides that snap**: lines follow the perspective guides, and more
  guide types arrive (three-point, isometric, curved, fisheye).
- **Sculpt modes** for the Line Editor: inflate, shrink, pinch, relax.
- **Pressure editing** with a curve, previewed before it applies.
- **Brush sandbox**: a scratch surface in Tool Properties to try a brush
  without touching the scene.
- **Pixel brushes** as a separate brush category.
- **3D rotation of a selection**, separate from its peg.

## Colour

The full plan is in [the colour system plan](../plans/colour-system.md).
Static colours stay the default; everything below is optional.

- **A perceptual picker** (OKLCH), so changing a colour's lightness
  doesn't change how saturated it looks. The current Square and Triangle
  pickers stay.
- **A preview of the result**: how the colour will look over what's
  under it, with the layer's blend mode and opacity.
- **Dynamic swatches**: a swatch that varies a little on its own
  (**Natural variation**), or changes with pressure, direction, tilt or
  speed, or blends several colours. The same stroke always gets the same
  colours, so reopening a scene, undoing or rendering never changes them.
- **Shadow and highlight suggestions** for the current colour, and a
  **target colour**: pick the result you want and get the colour to
  paint with for the current blend mode.
- **Colour Lab**: a separate view to try colours under a light on a
  sphere, cube, cloth or your own artwork, and save the ramp to the
  palette.
- **Linked palettes**: a skin colour whose shadow and highlight follow
  it when you change it, and day, sunset and night versions of a palette.

## Timing

- **Sound scrubbing**: hear the sound as you drag the playhead, then
  scratching forwards and backwards.
- **Temporal selection**: select across frames and layers and edit them
  together, with a preview of what changes.

## Animation and rigs

- **Library**: templates and symbols, and drawing substitution by role
  (mouth shapes, hands).
- **Curve, Envelope and other deformers**.
- **Multi-bone weights**: artwork influenced by several bones, with weight
  painting.
- **Reusable rigs**: save a skeleton as a template, change its
  proportions, and reuse animation.
- **Retargeting**: move animation between characters with different
  proportions, keeping planted hands and feet.

## Smart Charts

Smart Charts are the base for all of Symphony's tracking. Next:

- One stroke across several points.
- More things that follow a chart than pegs.
- **Interactive in-betweening**: in-betweens guided by charts, with
  matches you can edit, that produce ordinary editable drawings.
- Texture tracking and lighting that read the chart's motion.

## Nodes and compositing

- More effect nodes, towards Harmony's set.
- **Folders, groups and bundles** in the node graph, with exposed
  controls.
- **Performance view**: how long each node takes, to find what slows a
  scene down.
- **Adaptive preview quality**: lighter previews while you work, full
  quality in exports.
- **Mattes inside effects**, edited with one tool.

## Lighting and materials

- Height, normal and other material maps for artwork, painted or
  generated by hand-made rules.
- **Live 2D lighting**: directional, point and spot lights with cel or
  smooth shading.

## Remote workspace

Phones, tablets and other computers as extra panels over your local
network, through a web browser: no app, no account, no cloud.

- Pair a device by scanning a QR code. The connection is encrypted with a
  key from the code.
- Use a phone as a control surface: sliders, buttons, search.
- **Draw on a tablet**: an iPad with Apple Pencil or an Android tablet
  with S Pen shows the Drawing view, with pressure, and strokes go into
  the scene on your computer.

## Projects and production

- **Reference board**: a dockable board for reference images and notes.
- **Shared libraries** of drawings, rigs, brushes and palettes across
  projects, with updates you can accept or reject.
- A **project and scene browser**, and **scene setup** after creation.
- **Diagnostics**: one report for missing files, broken connections and
  slow nodes.
- Full **Harmony scene conversion**: artwork, palettes, rigs and effects.
- **Scripting**, with an API close to Harmony's.
- **Scanned drawings**: import and register paper animation.
- A shared multi-user database like Harmony Server: last, and optional.

## Pens and platforms

- Pressure and tilt on **Windows** (Windows Ink and Wintab, switchable)
  and **Linux** (Wayland and X11).
- Code-signed downloads, so the first-launch warning goes away.

## AI

Symphony will never include generative AI. If optional assisted tools are
ever added (for example, suggesting normal maps), they'll run on your
computer, be off by default, be labelled, keep their results editable,
and respect the **Use no AI** switch. See the [AI policy](../AI-POLICY.md).
